# MARGIN — 10-Minute Weekly Ritual

**Paid Sunday or payday. One page. Then close the tab.**

Product: MARGIN — Reimagine the next 90 days  
R.C.S. Creary · free lead magnet (Situations sliders are in the paid instrument only)

---

## Before you start (30 seconds)

- Open your MARGIN instrument (or a notes page if you haven’t bought yet).
- Have last week’s bank / card activity nearby — not a full budget rebuild.

---

## Checklist (≈10 minutes)

### 1. Add new charges (3 min)

Scan the week. Any new recurring or “almost recurring” spend?

- [ ] Wrote each new charge with name + amount + cadence
- [ ] Marked verdict: **Keep** · **Review** · **Cut**

### 2. Kill one Cut (4 min)

Look at items already marked **Cut**.

- [ ] Chose **one** to cancel or pause this week
- [ ] Actually canceled / paused it (email, app, phone)
- [ ] Removed it from the list or left a “done” note

*One cut creates room. A list of intentions does not.*

### 3. Look at minus 20% income once (2 min)

Ask only: if take-home dropped ~20% for 90 days, what would still fit?

- [ ] Glanced at that picture (in the paid instrument: set Income change to −20%)
- [ ] Noted one bill that would need a conversation — no spiral, no spreadsheet marathon

### 4. Close the tab (1 min)

- [ ] Exported JSON backup if you changed anything important
- [ ] Closed the tab. Ritual over until next payday / Sunday.

---

## What this is not

Not a full budget overhaul. Not debt counseling. Not bank sync.  
If you cannot cover essentials, contact a nonprofit credit counselor — do not use this ritual as a substitute.
