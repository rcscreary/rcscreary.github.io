About

R.C.S. Creary

R.C.S. Creary is an independent author and systems researcher. The work moves between culture, technology, education, and story — how people keep dignity and agency inside systems that would flatten them.

Fiction spans romance, thriller, and picture books. Education nonfiction includes The Great Mismatch, with sequel The Great Rebuild in progress. Through Real Realize, the same questions become products, printables, and conversations for readers who want more than a passive feed.
